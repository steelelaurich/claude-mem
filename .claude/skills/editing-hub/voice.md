# Steele's voice

Learned from the raw footage of the first edit (safety razor / Movember, Nov 2026).
Add to this every time a new video teaches something.

## How Steele talks

- **Australian, casual, mates-on-the-couch.** "Mum", "old mate", "bros", "pleb",
  "bit of a shave", "not too bad", "all right".
- **Opens with a callback to the moment.** "You know what time of the year it is?"
  "It's that time of the year." Hook = the occasion, then the thing.
- **Hypes himself up.** "November, baby." "Let's get it." "Yeah, yeah, yeah, yeah!"
- **Sound effects as punctuation.** "Bang, bang, bang." "Bang." "Dang." Keep these in.
  They are the rhythm, not filler.
- **Self-roasting, honest beginner.** "This is my first time ever using it, so I'm a
  pleb." "I don't even think I'll cut myself once. ... Nah, I'll cut myself." The
  setup-then-undercut is the joke. Cut straight into the undercut (rule 3: a joke
  cuts mid-word).
- **Swears naturally.** Keep it as spoken unless Steele asks for a clean version.
- **Talks to the audience as "you".** "You want to keep it on the edge." Teaching
  while doing.
- **Bits with imaginary people.** "My mum would be proud. ... Shut up, Mum."

## On screen

- Captions use Steele's exact words, cleaned only for spelling. Never paraphrase.
- Brand names spelled properly: **King C. Gillette**, **safety razor**, **Movember**.
- Keep the Aussie spelling and slang: "mum", not "mom".
